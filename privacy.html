Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At fitisamust, we collect basic information to provide you with an optimal fitness tracking experience. This includes your name, email address, payment details (processed securely via our payment partners), and any health-related data you choose to log, such as weight, nutritional goals, and food logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain the member dashboard.
  • Calculate personalized macro and caloric goals based on your intake profile.
  • Securely authenticate your access and prevent fraud.

3. Data Security and Encryption

Your privacy is our priority. Your password is cryptographically hashed and never stored in plain text. All user interactions and stored health data are kept strictly confidential and secure. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the fitisamust platform constitutes acceptance of any changes.
